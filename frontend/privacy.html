LEGAL DISCLOSURE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • CogniStudy AI Student Edition

1. Information We Collect

CogniStudy AI collects student account information including full name, student email address, college or university name, course of study, and hashed authentication credentials.

We also process user-provided study inputs (such as lecture transcripts, notes, and study prompts) to synthesize summaries and flashcard decks.

2. AI Processing and Third-Party Providers

Note summarization and flashcard generation requests are transmitted securely from our server to Google Gemini API models. All calls are executed server-side; API keys are never transmitted to student browsers.

We do not sell personal student data or use submitted lecture notes to train commercial advertising models.

3. Data Storage & Development Limitations

During local development and preview deployments, student data and session tokens are isolated per account and stored in server memory. This data is temporary and resets when the server restarts until a permanent database is connected.

4. Student Rights & Account Deletion

Students may update their profile details at any time via the Profile Settings screen, export their notes and flashcards in JSON or text formats, or request account deletion.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or technical questions regarding your study data, contact support via our Help Center.
