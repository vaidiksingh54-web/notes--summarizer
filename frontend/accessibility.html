INCLUSION & ACCESS

Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: October 2026

Our Commitment

CogniStudy AI is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all students, including those with visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor disabilities.

Implemented Accessibility Features

  • High-Contrast Dark Theme: Crisp text contrast ratios conforming to WCAG AA guidelines against deep background surfaces.
  • Full Keyboard Operability: Interactive elements (modals, flashcards, tabs, and quiz answer options) can be navigated via Tab and Enter/Space keys.
  • Reduced Motion Support: Media queries respect system preferences for users sensitive to animations.
  • Semantic HTML: Proper landmark elements, form labels, and ARIA attributes for screen reader compatibility.

Feedback & Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on any screen, please let us know via our Help Center.
