STUDENT HELP DESK

Help Center & Feature Guide

Explore how to use CogniStudy AI to transform your academic material into high-yield summaries, 3D active-recall flashcards, and exam-readiness quizzes.

📝

1. AI Notes Generator & Rich Editor

Paste raw lecture text or upload markdown/text documents up to 25,000 characters. Click Generate Notes to run server-side Gemini synthesis. Once generated:

  • Use the AI Tools menu to Shorten, Expand, Simplify (ELI5), or format into bullets.
  • Format headings, bold definitions, or lists using the built-in rich toolbar.
  • Click Save to Library or download as a .txt file.
  • Convert directly into flashcards or quizzes with single-click conversion buttons.
🃏

2. Active Recall 3D Flashcards

Enter any academic subject or pass an existing note to generate conceptual question-and-answer flashcards:

  • Click any card to flip between front (question) and back (in-depth explanation).
  • Mark cards as Mastered to advance your deck mastery progress percentage.
  • Use Shuffle or Restart to test your retention under varied sequence orders.
🕸

3. Visual AI Mind Map Maker

Visualize complex subjects and interconnected knowledge hierarchies:

  • Enter any topic or paste detailed notes to generate an interactive bilateral radial tree.
  • Click any node to open the Concept Inspector with detailed explanations and study insights.
  • Click the +/- collapse indicators to collapse or expand sub-branches for focused revision.
  • Use smooth drag-to-pan, mouse-wheel zoom, or the toolbar controls (+, -, 100%, Fit View).
  • Export high-resolution PNG or SVG images, or save directly to your student library.
🧠

4. Comprehensive Self-Testing Quizzes

Select between 5 or 10 questions to test theoretical and applied comprehension:

  • Track elapsed time with the built-in quiz timer.
  • Receive a complete percentage score breakdown upon submission.
  • Review question-by-question explanations with highlighted correct answers and rationales.
📅

5. Google Calendar Study Session Sync

Every study tool includes a Schedule in Google Calendar button. It creates a pre-populated 45-minute revision block with study topic summaries and actionable review goals.
