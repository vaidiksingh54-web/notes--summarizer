Academic Integrity & AI Disclaimer

Policy Guidelines for Students and Educators

1. Study Aid Purpose

CogniStudy AI is created strictly as a comprehension, revision, and active recall study aid. It is intended to assist students in summarizing their own lecture notes and testing their personal understanding with practice flashcards and self-administered quizzes.

2. Prohibition of Unauthorized Exam Use

CogniStudy AI must not be used during proctored exams, closed-book assessments, or in any manner that breaches your university, college, or school's code of academic conduct.

3. AI Output Verification Notice

Generative AI models synthesize information based on statistical language patterns. While tailored for academic accuracy, AI output can contain omissions or inaccuracies. Students must verify formulas, mathematical derivations, medical dosages, or legal citations against primary academic textbooks and peer-reviewed journals.
