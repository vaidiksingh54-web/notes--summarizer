    The philosophy behind NoteWise AI

    Make studying feel lighter & smarter.

    NoteWise AI is built around a single, student-centered objective: eliminate the mental fatigue of reading endless unorganized notes, transforming them into high-retention summaries and practice tests.

    Project objective Transform dense lecture notes into bite-sized summaries and active recall quizzes.
    Target users College and high school students preparing for exams, tests, and seminars.
    Key benefits Saves study time · Enhances recall · Simulates exam scenarios · Keeps notes organized.
    Technology status Current version: Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript frontend prototype.
    ✦

    Future Product Roadmap: Real cloud authentication (Firebase Auth), database persistence (Cloud Firestore), and live AI inference (Gemini API) will be integrated in the backend phase.

    Frequently asked questions

    Good to know.

    Everything you need to know about the NoteWise AI frontend prototype.

    What is NoteWise AI? +

    NoteWise AI is a frontend prototype designed to streamline revision by turning complex notes into structured summaries and interactive quizzes.

    How does the Quiz Generator work? +

    In this prototype, the Quiz Generator analyzes topic keywords from your pasted notes and generates targeted multiple-choice questions from an internal question bank with score calculation and answer explanations.

    Which file formats are supported? +

    You can drop real .txt and .md files into the Summarizer, and they will be read immediately in your browser via the HTML5 FileReader API. PDF and DOCX formats will be processed server-side via pdf-parse and Mammoth in the upcoming backend release.

    Is my data stored securely online? +

    This prototype runs strictly in your browser. Demo profile information and notes are stored locally in temporary browser memory and localStorage. No credentials or notes leave your computer.

    Is this connected to a live AI API? +

    Not yet. This is an honest frontend prototype showcasing user workflows, layout, animations, and interactive testing. A secure backend with Gemini AI integration is scheduled for the next phase.
